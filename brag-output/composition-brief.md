# Hyperframes Composition Brief: CoD4 Monitor

## Objective
A 25s polished presentation video for CoD4 Monitor, built from the real screenshots in `docs/brag/`.

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output/composition/`; render: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Landscape 1920x1080, 25s

## Source Material
- Files read: `docs/brag/README.md`, the `docs/brag/*.png` screenshots, `ui/src/app.css`, `ui/public/fonts`
- Screens used: desktop-02-live, desktop-06-player-profile, desktop-20-moderation, mobile-01-live, mobile-03-player-profile, mobile-07-moderation (copied to `assets/shots/`)
- Copy (from the tour README): "Built for phones first", "Ban evasion detection", "One page per player", "Free, open-source", "one Go binary + SQLite"

## Creative Direction
Polished preset. Each scene shows one screen. The text column sits on the left, the app window on the right, and the window slowly punches in on its key detail. Soft crossfades between scenes. See `brag-plan.md` for the storyboard.
Avoid generic SaaS language, abstract filler, and any restyling of the screenshots.

## Visual Identity
bg #0B0E13, surface #141922, line #263042, text #E8ECF1, muted #9AA6B5, accent #E5484D. Display font: Space Grotesk; body: Manrope (local woff2).

## Audio
- Music: `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.32, with fades in and out
- Cues: bundled preset. Locks at 8.74, 13.11, 22.93; phones on the beat grid at 17.47 / 18.56 / 19.66
- SFX: impactSoft_medium_001 on scene changes, drop_001 when the hook window lands, card-slide-1 per phone, impactBell_heavy_000 on the logo. Volume 0.55–0.7.
- Audio-reactive: intentionally none (polished restraint)
